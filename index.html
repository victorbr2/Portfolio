Solar Drive

Interactive experience

Solo project · Windows, Web, Android

An interactive Solar System experience: pilot a spaceship between planets, moons and asteroids. Fly close to any body and the camera pulls back to show it whole, next to a data card with its radius, mass, gravity, orbit and more.

Includes the Sun, the eight planets, major moons and the asteroid belt, a system map with route guidance and a route compass, and data from NASA NSSDCA and NASA/JPL. Playable with keyboard, gamepad or touch, in Catalan, Spanish and English.

Unity Educational Games UI/UX Design Data-Driven Content Touch Controls Localization

Potion Grabber

Featured project

Solo project · 3 months · PC, Web, Android

An incremental game developed from scratch in Unity 6. Steal valuable potions, sell your loot at the end of each round and reinvest your earnings in upgrades.

I designed and built the core systems: a 44-node upgrade tree split across 4 branches, the game economy and its balancing, and the full UI. Upgrade effects are data-driven through ScriptableObjects, so mechanics can be reused, rebalanced and extended with new content.

Unity 6 C# Progression Design Game Economy UI Development ScriptableObjects
