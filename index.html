A little about me

Art meets technology.

My approach

I'm a game developer focused on the intersection of technical art, visual design and interactive systems. I enjoy finding practical ways to bring creative ideas to life, from shaping user interfaces and visual experiences to building the tools and systems that support them.

Most of my projects have been developed independently from the ground up, giving me the opportunity to work across different stages of development and understand how art, design and technology come together. Through Bugzonia, I've also gained experience collaborating within a team, focusing on production, UI/UX, technical art and technical implementation.

I'm interested in opportunities where I can contribute to the visual quality, usability and technical foundations of games while continuing to grow as a Technical Artist.
